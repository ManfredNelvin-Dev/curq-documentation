# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to the CURQ Frequently Asked Questions page. Here you can find answers to common questions about using CURQ.

---

## 1. How do I switch between different business modules in CURQ?
You can easily switch between modules using the left-hand navigation sidebar. Click on any module title (such as **Accounting**, **CRM**, **Sales**, or **Inventory**) to expand its submenu options, including Overview, Manuals, Configurations, Articles, and FAQs.

---

## 2. How do I export reports and documentation from CURQ?
Reports across modules (such as Accounting financial statements or Sales analytics) can be exported directly from their respective list and pivot views. Click the **Export** icon or select rows and click **Action > Export** to download your data in Excel or CSV format.

---

## 3. What should I do if a user loses access or forgets their password?
An administrator can navigate to **Settings > Users & Companies > Users**, locate the user's profile, and click **Send Password Reset Instructions**. Alternatively, administrators can reset passwords directly from the user form.

---

## 4. How does CURQ handle multi-currency transactions?
CURQ supports multi-currency operations seamlessly. You can enable multi-currency under **Accounting > Settings**, configure live or manual exchange rates, and create invoices, bills, or journal entry vouchers in foreign currencies with automatic gain/loss reconciliation.

---

## 5. How can I customize user permissions and access rights?
Access rights are managed via security groups in **Settings > Users**. Each user can be assigned specific access levels (such as *User*, *Manager*, or *Administrator*) per module, ensuring strict data governance and role-based access control.
