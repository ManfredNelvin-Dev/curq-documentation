# Timesheet FAQ

Frequently asked questions about creating timesheet sheets, tracking working hours with the live timer, submission workflows, managerial approvals, and reporting in CURQ Timesheets.

---

## How do I create and submit a timesheet sheet?
Open the **Timesheets** application and navigate to **My Timesheets > My Timesheet Sheets**. Click **New**, select the relevant project and task deliverable, and record your working hours under the **Details** tab or directly within the weekly matrix grid. Review the recorded hours in the **Summary** tab to verify that totals align with your workweek schedule, and click **Submit to Reviewer** to lock the sheet and route it for manager validation.

## What is the difference between My Timesheets and My Timesheets to Submit?
**My Timesheets** displays all your individual, atomic timesheet records, allowing you to log, inspect, filter, and group granular time entries across dates and tasks. **My Timesheets to Submit** is an operational staging queue displaying only unsubmitted hours and periodic sheets awaiting submission. Once you click **Submit to Reviewer** on a sheet, its records automatically disappear from the submission queue. If a reviewer refuses the sheet, the records immediately reappear there for correction.

## How do I track my working hours in real time?
Use the **Start Work** interactive timer. Open **Timesheets > Start work** from the top navigation bar, enter a brief work description, and select the target project and deliverable task. Click **Start new timer** to track elapsed time in the background while you work across CURQ applications. When the activity is complete, stop the timer; CURQ automatically converts the session duration into a verified timesheet entry and synchronizes it with your active weekly sheet.

## What happens when a reviewer approves or refuses a timesheet sheet?
Reviewers authorized under your organization's configured review policy (*HR Officers, HR Managers, or Timesheets Managers*) inspect submitted sheets under **To Review > Timesheet Sheets to Review**. When approved, entries are permanently locked against modifications to preserve payroll integrity and labor costs are validated on project analytic accounts. When refused, the sheet status reverts to `Open`, unlocking entries and returning them to the employee's **My Timesheets to Submit** queue alongside manager feedback logged in the chatter.

## How can managers view total hours spent across projects, employees, or tasks?
Navigate to **Timesheets > Reporting** and select an analytical view: **By Employee**, **By Project**, **By Task**, or **By Billing Type**. Use the **Measures** selector to analyze total time spent, billable hours, non-billable time, margins, or labor costs, and utilize dynamic grouping and filters to evaluate team utilization and project burn rates.
