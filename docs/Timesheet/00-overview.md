# Timesheet

Record employee working hours, track project time allocation, manage customer billable hours, and analyze time logs in CURQ 18.

## Efficient Time Tracking Across Projects and Tasks
With Timesheet, capture and track time spent by employees and contractors on individual projects, tasks, or customer service tickets. Monitor real-time progress, compare planned vs. actual effort, and simplify operational reporting.

## Seamless Integration with Project and Invoicing
CURQ Timesheet integrates seamlessly with Project and Sales, allowing billable hours to be converted automatically into customer invoices while providing management with clear visibility over team utilization.
