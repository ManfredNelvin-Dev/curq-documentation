# Timesheet FAQ

Frequently asked questions about creating timesheet sheets, tracking working hours with the live timer, submission workflows, managerial approvals, and reporting in CURQ 18.

---

### 1. How do I create and submit a timesheet sheet?

Open the **Timesheets** app and navigate to **My Timesheets > My Timesheet Sheets**. Click **+ New**, select the relevant project and task, and add your working hours under the **Details** tab or directly on the weekly matrix grid. Review the recorded hours in the **Summary** tab and click **Submit to Reviewer** when the timesheet sheet is ready for review. For complete instructions, see [Creating and Submitting Timesheet Sheets](./manual/01-create-and-submit-timesheets.md).

---

### 2. What is the difference between My Timesheets and My Timesheets to Submit?

**My Timesheets** displays your individual, line-by-line timesheet entries, allowing you to view, record, and filter granular time spent on specific projects and tasks. **My Timesheets to Submit** displays periodic timesheet sheets that still need to be submitted for review. Once you submit a timesheet sheet using the **Submit to Reviewer** button, it automatically disappears from the pending submission list. Refer to [My Timesheets to Submit](./manual/02-my-timesheets-to-submit.md) and [My Timesheets](./manual/03-my-timesheets.md) for details.

---

### 3. How can I track my working hours automatically?

Use the **Start Work** feature to start a real-time timer for an active activity. Enter a description and select the relevant project and task. The timer records elapsed time while you work. Stop the timer when you finish the activity; CURQ 18 automatically converts the recorded duration into a verified timesheet entry. For full details, see [Starting and Managing a Work Timer](./manual/05-start-and-manage-work-timer.md).

---

### 4. Who can approve or reject timesheet sheets?

Authorized reviewers, such as HR Officers, HR Managers, or Timesheets Managers, can review submitted timesheet sheets according to your organization's configured **Timesheet Sheet Review Policy**. Reviewers can approve a sheet when all entries are valid or reject it with feedback in the chatter if corrections are required. For guidance on auditing and validation, see [Timesheet Sheets to Review](./manual/06-timesheet-sheets-to-review.md).

---

### 5. How can I view the total hours spent on employees, projects, or tasks?

Open **Timesheets → Reporting** and select the appropriate report, such as **Timesheets by Employee**, **Timesheets by Project**, **Timesheets by Task**, or **Timesheets by Billing Type**. Use the **Measures** selector, date filters, and multi-level grouping options to analyze recorded working hours across teams, milestones, or reporting periods. For a comprehensive walkthrough, see [Analyzing Timesheet Reports](./manual/07-analyze-timesheet-reports.md).
