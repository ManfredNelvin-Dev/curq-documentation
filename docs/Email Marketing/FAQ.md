# Email Marketing FAQ

Frequently asked questions about outgoing mail server configuration, mailing lists, campaign scheduling, performance tracking, and blacklisted email addresses in CURQ 18.

---

### 1. How do I configure Email Marketing to send emails?

To send email marketing campaigns, configure a dedicated outgoing SMTP mail server. Navigate to Configuration → Settings, enable the Dedicated Server option, and select an existing outgoing mail server or configure a new one. Verify the configuration by sending a test email before launching a campaign.

---

### 2. What is the difference between a mailing list and a mailing?

A Mailing List is a group of contacts who can receive marketing communications. A Mailing is an individual email created and sent to selected recipients. You can use the same mailing list for multiple mailings.

---

### 3. How do I schedule an email marketing campaign?

Open the mailing you want to send, complete the recipient and email content details, and click Schedule. Select the desired sending date and time, then confirm the schedule. CURQ queues the mailing for delivery at the scheduled time.

---

### 4. How can I track the performance of my email campaigns?

Navigate to Email Marketing → Reporting to analyze campaign performance. You can view metrics such as sent, delivered, opened, clicked, and bounced emails. Use filters, grouping options, and chart views to compare results and identify trends.

---

### 5. How can I prevent emails from being sent to unsubscribed contacts?

Use Configuration → Blacklisted Email Addresses to manage email addresses that must not receive further marketing communications. You can also configure opt-out reasons to collect feedback from recipients who unsubscribe. Always respect recipients' communication preferences when managing mailing lists and sending campaigns.
