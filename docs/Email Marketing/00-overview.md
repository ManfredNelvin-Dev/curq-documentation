# Email Marketing

Create newsletters and promotional campaigns, manage customer mailing lists, track campaign engagement, and configure dedicated mail servers in CURQ 18.

## Engaging Communication Campaigns
With Email Marketing, design and broadcast mass mailings directly to curated subscriber lists. Monitor delivery rates, open percentages, link clickthroughs, and unsubscribes from a unified dashboard.

## Dedicated Outgoing Server Integration
CURQ Email Marketing supports dedicated SMTP mail server integration to isolate mass marketing broadcasts from transactional business emails, ensuring optimal deliverability and domain sender reputation.
