# Inventory FAQ

Frequently asked technical, operational, and architectural questions regarding CURQ Inventory, warehouse operation types, stock picking lifecycles, outbound delivery validation, and replenishment rules.

---

### 1. What does red text indicate in the Scheduled Date column?
In both the Receipts and Delivery Orders registers, dates displayed in red text *(e.g., `18 days ago`, `6 days ago`)* indicate that the scheduled arrival or dispatch deadline has passed. Warehouse managers prioritize these overdue transfers to prevent supplier supply bottlenecks or customer SLA breaches.

---

### 2. What is the difference between "Demand" and "Quantity" on a transfer?
* **Demand**: The baseline quantity of products requested by the originating document *(such as a Customer Sales Order or Vendor Purchase Order)*.
* **Quantity**: The actual physical count of units picked, counted, and staged at the dock by warehouse operators. When Quantity is lower than Demand upon validation, CURQ automatically prompts operators to create a Backorder for remaining units or proceed without backorder.

---

### 3. What does "Product Availability: Available" mean in green text?
On an outbound Delivery Order, **Available** in green text confirms that all requested product lines have been physically located and reserved in warehouse storage *(e.g., `WH/Stock`)*. The picking team can safely pick and pack the goods without risk of stock collision with other orders.

---

### 4. How does the double-entry inventory model work in CURQ?
CURQ Inventory treats all stock handling as balanced double-entry moves between source and destination locations. Products are never generated or deleted arbitrarily. Inbound receipts move stock from `Partner Locations/Vendors` to `WH/Stock`, internal transfers move stock between shelves, and outbound deliveries move stock from `WH/Stock` to `Partner Locations/Customers`.

---

### 5. What happens when I click the "Validate" button on a delivery order?
Clicking **Validate** executes several simultaneous enterprise transactions:
1. Deducts physical product units from the internal warehouse storage location *(e.g., `WH/Stock`)*.
2. Posts balanced stock move records to the inventory valuation ledger.
3. Updates the delivery status chevron from **Ready** to **Done**.
4. Automatically updates connected Sales Order lines to `Delivered`, unlocking customer invoice generation in CURQ Accounting.

---

### 6. Can I process direct supplier shipments to customers without warehouse intake?
Yes. The **Dropship** operation card coordinates direct vendor-to-customer deliveries. In dropshipping, the goods move directly from `Partner Locations/Vendors` to `Partner Locations/Customers`, eliminating warehouse handling costs and dock dwell time.

---

### 7. How do automated reordering rules prevent warehouse stockouts?
Reordering rules track forecasted inventory balances *(Quantity On Hand + Incoming Receipts - Outgoing Deliveries)*. When forecasted stock dips below the configured **Minimum Quantity**, CURQ automatically generates a draft Purchase Order or Request for Quotation (RFQ) to replenish inventory up to the **Maximum Quantity**.

---

### 8. How do I print warehouse picking sheets and delivery slips?
On any active receipt or delivery order form:
1. Click the **Print** button in the top action bar.
2. Select **Delivery Slip** or **Picking Operations**.
3. CURQ generates a PDF document including product names, quantities, source locations, and barcode labels for physical warehouse execution.

---

### 9. How do I audit the financial inventory valuation entries generated by warehouse transfers?
On any completed transfer in **Done** status *(such as Inbound Receipt `WH/IN/00070` or Delivery Order `WH/OUT/00069`)*, click the **Valuation** smart button located in the top control bar. CURQ opens the **Stock Valuation** register filtered to that transfer, displaying the execution timestamp, reference code, product SKU, signed quantity delta *(positive `+4.00` for vendor receipts adding to warehouse inventory; negative `-1.00` for customer dispatches reducing stock)*, and total monetary balance sheet adjustment.

---

### 10. Where can warehouse operators verify source picking locations and serial numbers?
On any transfer form, click the **Moves** smart button in the header to open the **Detailed Operations** view. For outbound delivery orders, this table displays the source storage bin in the **Pick From** column *(e.g., `WH/Stock`)*. For inbound supplier receipts, it displays assigned hardware tracking identifiers in the **Lot/Serial Number** column alongside the moved **Quantity** and applied **Unit of Measure**.

---

### 11. What does the "Goods" search filter mean in the Products catalog?
When navigating to **Inventory > Products > Products**, CURQ applies the `Goods` filter tag by default. This filter restricts catalog listings to tangible physical goods and consumable materials that undergo physical stock tracking, excluding intangible service products that do not require warehouse storage.

---

### 12. What is the operational difference between "On Hand" and "Forecasted" quantities?
* **On Hand**: The physical quantity of product units currently stored in internal warehouse bins *(e.g., `WH/Stock`)*.
* **Forecasted**: The projected future stock availability calculated by adding incoming purchase receipts and subtracting outbound customer delivery reservations from on-hand stock. Warehouse leads rely on forecasted quantities to schedule replenishment before customer orders create stockouts.

---

### 13. What does the "In: 1 / Out: 0" indicator represent on a product form?
The In/Out smart indicator in the product header displays real-time cumulative warehouse physical movements for the item. The **In** value *(e.g., `In: 1`)* represents total units received into internal warehouse storage across completed inbound receipts. The **Out** value *(e.g., `Out: 0`)* represents total units dispatched to customers on validated outbound delivery orders. Clicking this control opens the filtered stock move history for the product.

---

### 14. Why should inventory products use an Invoicing Policy of "Delivered quantities"?
Setting the invoicing policy to **Delivered quantities** aligns financial revenue recognition with physical warehouse fulfillment. Under this policy, CURQ Accounting prevents generating a customer invoice until warehouse operators validate the outbound Delivery Order in **Done** status. This eliminates customer billing discrepancies resulting from delayed dispatches, partial fulfillments, or unexpected inventory shortages.

---

### 15. What is the function of Counterpart Locations on the product form?
Under CURQ's double-entry inventory framework, every stock movement requires balanced source and destination locations. The counterpart locations on the product form define default virtual partners:
* **Production Location**: Serves as the virtual counterpart location *(e.g., `Virtual Locations/Production`)* when raw materials are consumed or finished goods are produced.
* **Inventory Location**: Serves as the virtual counterpart location *(e.g., `Virtual Locations/Inventory adjustment`)* when cycle counts or stock audits record surpluses or shrinkage.

---

### 16. How do "Description for Receipts" and "Description for Delivery Orders" assist dock operations?
These specialized fields on the product's **Inventory** tab allow catalog managers to communicate operational instructions directly to warehouse operators:
* **Description for Receipts**: Automatically prints on inbound Receiving Slips and picking operations sheets, guiding receiving workers on putaway bin destinations, hazardous handling, or climate-controlled storage.
* **Description for Delivery Orders**: Automatically prints on outbound Delivery Slips, guiding shipping teams on fragile parcel packing, protective wrapping, or customs documentation.

---

### 17. What information must be filled when manually creating a Lot or Serial Number?
When creating a serial number at **Inventory > Products > Lots / Serial Numbers > + New**, two fields are mandatory:
1. **Lot/Serial Number**: The unique alphanumeric string or serial code *(e.g., `0000001`)*.
2. **Product**: The specific catalog item the serial identifier belongs to *(e.g., `Fair Phone 5`)*. Without selecting a product, the record cannot be saved.
Optional fields include **Internal Reference**, **Cost**, and batch remarks in the **Description** tab. Fields such as **On Hand Quantity**, **Total Value**, and **Average Cost** are system-computed and update automatically when goods are received into warehouse storage.

---

### 18. What is the operational difference between a Product Template and a Product Variant?
* **Product Template**: Represents the parent catalog concept *(e.g., `Fair Phone 5`)* with global parameters such as product type, base invoicing policies, and logistics supply routes.
* **Product Variant**: Represents a specific SKU permutation *(e.g., `Fair Phone 5, Color: Black`)* with distinct attribute values, independent selling prices *(e.g., `€ 209.00` vs `€ 199.00`)*, unique barcode identifiers, and separate on-hand inventory levels.

---

### 19. How do I inspect the graphical supply chain route for a product in CURQ Inventory?
Open any product form *(e.g., `Fair Phone 5`)*, click the **Inventory** notebook tab, and locate the **Operations** section. Click the **-> View Diagram** link located next to the route checkboxes. CURQ renders the **Product Routes Report**, displaying a visual flowchart of stock movements across `Partners/Vendors`, `WH/Stock`, and `Partners/Customers` with active inbound receipts and outbound delivery paths.

---

### 20. What is the operational difference between a Push Rule and a Pull Rule in product routes?
* **Push Rule**: An automatic forwarding rule where incoming goods arriving at a source location are immediately pushed forward to a destination location regardless of downstream demand *(such as auto-moving goods from dock receiving to quality inspection)*.
* **Pull Rule**: A demand-driven fulfillment rule where a need at a destination location *(such as a confirmed customer sales order)* pulls stock from an upstream source location *(such as `WH/Stock`)*, triggering automated procurement or picking tasks.

---

### 21. How do I perform a physical inventory count and stock adjustment in CURQ Inventory?
Navigate to **Inventory > Operations > Physical Inventory**. In the adjustments register, review the theoretical **On Hand Quantity** and enter the physical count verified on warehouse shelves into the **Counted Quantity** column. Review the calculated **Difference**. Click the row-level **Apply** button to commit an individual adjustment, or click **Apply All** in the top action bar to commit all counts across the warehouse. CURQ automatically posts balanced double-entry moves against `Virtual Locations/Inventory adjustment`.

---

### 22. What is the operational difference between "Apply", "Set", and "Clear" on an adjustment line?
* **Apply**: Commits the entered physical count variance, updating on-hand warehouse inventory balances and posting financial valuation entries.
* **Set**: Sets the **Counted Quantity** to match the theoretical **On Hand Quantity**, confirming that physical stock exactly matches system records without discrepancy.
* **Clear**: Clears the entered counted quantity, resetting the field so the line remains pending without applying any inventory changes.

---

### 23. What information is required to process a Scrap Order?
On the Scrap Order creation form (**Inventory > Operations > Scrap > + New**), only two fields are mandatory:
1. **Product**: The inventory catalog item to be scrapped *(e.g., `Fair Phone 5 (Black)`)*.
2. **Quantity**: The physical count of units being written off *(e.g., `2.00 Units`)*.
Supporting optional fields include **Scrap Reason** *(documenting transit damage, defect, or expiration)*, **Source Document** *(linking to a receipt or quality ticket)*, and the **Replenish Quantities** checkbox. Clicking the dark blue **Validate** button commits the write-off and removes the units from warehouse storage.

---

### 24. What does the "Replenish Quantities" checkbox do on a Scrap Order?
When checked, **Replenish Quantities** instructs CURQ to immediately schedule replenishment for the scrapped amount. Rather than waiting for regular periodic reordering runs, CURQ triggers an automated procurement action or draft Purchase Order to restore inventory balances for the discarded units.

---

### 25. Where can warehouse auditors verify the source location and scrap destination of a written-off product?
On any completed Scrap Order in **Done** status *(e.g., `SP/00001`)*, click the **Product Moves** smart button in the header. CURQ opens the **Moves History** register filtered to that scrap document, displaying the **From** source warehouse storage location *(e.g., `WH/Stock`)*, the **To** destination virtual scrap location *(e.g., `Virtual Locations/Scrap`)*, and the decremented quantity in red text *(e.g., `2.00 Units`)*.

---

### 26. What status pipeline does an individual stock move follow in CURQ Inventory?
When inspecting an individual stock move record, the chevron bar tracks operational movement through five stages:
1. **New**: The movement record has been initialized in draft status.
2. **Waiting Availability**: The move is waiting for physical stock to be received into storage or unreserved from competing orders.
3. **Partially Available**: A fraction of requested units is physically available in the source storage bin.
4. **Available**: All required units are physically reserved in storage and ready for transfer execution.
5. **Done**: The movement has been physically executed, transferring units into the destination location and finalizing valuation postings.

---

### 27. How does the Replenishment board calculate the "To Order" quantity?
CURQ calculates the **To Order** quantity based on the item's live **Forecast**, configured **Min** (safety stock minimum), and configured **Max** (target capacity ceiling):
* When the forecasted quantity drops strictly below the minimum threshold (`Forecast < Min`), CURQ triggers a replenishment proposition.
* The system calculates the suggested order quantity as `To Order = Max - Forecast`.
* For example, for an item with `Forecast = 0.00`, `Min = 10.00`, and `Max = 50.00`, CURQ recommends ordering `50.00 Units` to replenish warehouse inventory back to the maximum storage limit.

---

### 28. What is the operational difference between "Order" and "Order To Max" on the Replenishment board?
* **Order**: Dispatches replenishment orders for the static quantity currently displayed in the **To Order** column. If inventory conditions change between page load and execution, the quantity remains fixed.
* **Order To Max**: Forces CURQ to perform a dynamic re-evaluation of demand and stock reservations at the exact moment of execution, adjusting the order quantity to ensure the placed order brings forecasted inventory precisely to the configured **Max** limit.

---

### 29. What happens when clicking "Automate" on a replenishment rule line?
Clicking the **Automate** action button (cycle arrows icon) removes the rule from the manual replenishment review board and converts it to an automated scheduler rule. Once automated, the rule executes during background scheduler cycles without requiring human confirmation, automatically generating draft Purchase Orders in CURQ Purchase or Manufacturing Orders in CURQ Manufacturing whenever stock falls below the minimum limit.

---

### 30. How does the Horizon filter affect replenishment propositions?
The **HORIZON** filter (such as `0 days`, `7 days`, or `30 days`) controls the forward-looking forecasting window used by CURQ to project inventory requirements:
* At `0 days`, CURQ only flags products that have existing stock deficits or pending delivery commitments requiring immediate replenishment today.
* At expanded horizons (such as `30 days`), CURQ incorporates scheduled future delivery orders, confirmed sales commitments, and bill of materials component demands that mature within the selected timeframe, enabling procurement teams to prevent upcoming stock shortages in advance.

---

### 31. Why does CURQ display the warning "There is no matching vendor price to generate the purchase order" when ordering replenishment?
This warning occurs during replenishment order generation when CURQ cannot identify an applicable vendor pricelist entry on the product's **Purchase** tab. Common triggers include:
1. **No Vendor Defined**: No supplier is configured on the product record.
2. **Minimum Quantity Not Reached**: The primary vendor has a minimum order quantity threshold *(such as `100.00 Units` for `Acme Technologies`)* that exceeds the replenishment proposal *(such as `50.00 Units`)*. Because the purchase request does not meet the supplier's volume requirement, CURQ refuses to generate an invalid Purchase Order.
3. **Invalid Agreement Dates**: The vendor pricelist line carries an expired validity date range.
To resolve this, click **Edit Product** on the warning dialog and adjust the vendor's minimum quantity to `0.00` or `1.00`, or add a tiered pricing line that accommodates smaller replenishment orders.

---

### 32. What is the difference between "On received quantities" and "On ordered quantities" in Vendor Bills control policy?
* **On received quantities (3-Way Matching)**: Enforces physical inventory verification. CURQ prevents vendor bill validation until warehouse dock operators have verified and validated the physical receipt of goods (`WH/IN`). This protects against paying for damaged, short-shipped, or lost freight.
* **On ordered quantities (2-Way Matching)**: Permits accounting teams to post and pay vendor bills based solely on the agreed purchase order quantities before the goods arrive at the warehouse dock.

---

### 33. Why are some products missing when searching in the Product dropdown on a replenishment line?
When adding a manual reordering rule on the Replenishment board (**Inventory > Operations > Replenishment > + New**), CURQ applies strict domain filters that exclude non-eligible items:
1. **Product Type Filtering**: Only **Storable Products** (`type == 'product'`) qualify for reordering rules. Service items *(such as Delivery charges, Expenses, Mileage, Timesheet hours)* and Consumable items are automatically excluded because they do not track physical warehouse inventory or safety stock balances.
2. **Existing Rule Deduplication**: CURQ only allows one active reordering rule per product per warehouse location. If a product already has an active reordering rule assigned to `WH/Stock`, it is excluded from the lookup to prevent duplicate, conflicting safety rules.
3. **Active Procurement Routes**: Products must have an active supply route *(such as Buy or Manufacture)* configured on their Inventory tab to support replenishment order generation.

---

### 34. Where is the "Storable Product" option in CURQ Inventory?
In CURQ, the legacy 'Storable Product' classification is achieved by setting **Product Type** to **Goods** and checking the **Track Inventory** checkbox under the **General Information** tab:
* **When Track Inventory is unchecked**: The product functions as a **Consumable** (a physical material without on-hand stock tracking or safety stock rules).
* **When Track Inventory is checked**: The product becomes a tracked **Storable Product**. CURQ displays the **On Hand** and **Forecasted** smart buttons, activates safety stock reordering rules, and allows the product to appear in **Operations > Replenishment**.

---

### 35. What happens on the Replenishment board after clicking "Order"?
When clicking **Order** on an active replenishment line, CURQ executes several simultaneous updates:
1. **Toast Notification**: Displays a confirmation banner in the upper-right corner containing a direct link to the created document *(e.g., `The following replenishment order has been generated: P00132`)*.
2. **Forecast Projection Update**: Increases the **Forecast** quantity by the ordered amount *(e.g., from `0.00` to `50.00`)* with a facility icon indicating pending incoming intake.
3. **Shortfall Resolution**: Resets the **To Order** metric from its recommended volume back to `0.00`.
4. **Action Button Deactivation**: Automatically hides the row-level `Order` and `Automate` buttons, leaving only `Snooze` since no pending reorder requirement exists.

---

### 36. How does CURQ calculate the "Expected Arrival" date on a generated Purchase Order?
CURQ calculates **Expected Arrival** by adding the vendor's contracted **Delivery Lead Time** *(configured on the product's Purchase tab)* to the order placement timestamp:
* For example, if an RFQ is generated on `11/06/2026 17:30:00` for a vendor with a `1`-day lead time *(e.g., `Acme Technologies`)*, CURQ sets the **Expected Arrival** to `11/07/2026 17:30:00`.
* When the purchase order is confirmed, this expected date determines the scheduled receipt deadline on the resulting inbound warehouse receipt (`WH/IN`).

---

### 37. What does the Forecasted Report show and how is the stock equation calculated?
The **Forecasted Report** (`Operations > Replenishment > Forecast Graph Icon`) evaluates inventory availability over time using the dynamic balancing formula:
$$\text{On Hand} + \text{Incoming} - \text{Outgoing} = \text{Forecasted Units}$$
* **On Hand**: Physical inventory units physically stored within warehouse storage bins *(e.g., `50.00`)*.
* **Incoming**: Product quantities scheduled for delivery on confirmed purchase orders or production runs.
* **Outgoing**: Product quantities committed to confirmed customer sales delivery orders.
* **Forecasted**: The net available inventory projected for future fulfillment. The interactive step chart visualizes stock increases when receipts arrive and decreases when orders dispatch.

---

### 38. Why did the Incoming quantity drop to 0.00 after validating the vendor receipt?
Before receiving goods, purchase order quantities are classified as **Incoming** stock. When dock operators validate the inbound receipt transfer (`WH/IN`), physical units are officially received into warehouse storage (`WH/Stock`). CURQ transfers the quantity from **Incoming** to **On Hand** *(e.g., On Hand becomes `50.00`, Incoming becomes `0.00`)*, keeping net forecasted availability constant while acknowledging physical possession.

---

### 39. How do I generate an inventory stock valuation report as of a specific historical date?
Navigate to **Inventory > Reporting > Valuation**. In the top action bar, click the dark blue **Valuation at Date** button. In the modal dialog, enter the desired historical audit timestamp *(such as month-end closing or fiscal year cutoff)* and click **Confirm**. CURQ recalculates physical quantities and monetary asset values as they stood at that exact historical moment, rolling back subsequent receipts, deliveries, and adjustments.

---

### 40. Why does the Stock Valuation register display a Total Value of €0.00 when physical quantity is 50.00?
Stock valuation is calculated by multiplying physical inventory by the product's standard or moving-average cost price recorded on the product master. If the product record had its **Cost** initialized at `€ 0.00`, the valuation ledger computes `50.00 * € 0.00 = € 0.00`. Under automated inventory valuation, posting the matching vendor bill in CURQ Accounting or adjusting the standard cost on the product form reconciles the inventory asset balance sheet account with the actual acquisition price *(e.g., `50.00 * € 99.00 = € 4,950.00`)*.

---

### 41. Why does a Stock Valuation line appear with a Quantity of 0.00 and a positive Total Value?
A valuation row displaying **`Quantity: 0.00`** and a positive or negative **`Total Value`** represents an automated inventory cost revaluation rather than a physical movement:
* When a product's standard cost is updated *(e.g., from `€ 0.00` to `€ 99.00`)* or when price variances are reconciled against vendor bills, CURQ adjusts the general ledger inventory asset valuation for all on-hand units.
* Because physical units stored in warehouse bins remain unchanged *(e.g., `50.00 Units`)*, CURQ logs `Quantity: 0.00` alongside the pure financial valuation delta *(e.g., `+€ 4,950.00`)*.
* The summary footer correctly reflects net physical units *(50.00)* and net financial balance sheet asset value *(€ 4,950.00)*.

---

### 42. What is the difference between Moves History and Moves Analysis in CURQ Inventory?
* **Moves History** (**Reporting > Moves History**): Provides a tabular, line-by-line audit ledger of every atomic stock move between source and destination locations, optimized for auditing specific lot numbers, picking timestamps, and individual operator accountability.
* **Moves Analysis** (**Reporting > Moves Analysis**): Provides a high-level analytical business intelligence suite equipped with multidimensional pivot matrices, comparative demand bar charts, and operational fulfillment ratios across operation types *(Receipts, Deliveries, Scraps)* and calendar periods.

---

### 43. Why does the "None" column appear in the Moves Analysis pivot view?
In the Moves Analysis pivot view, the **None** column captures physical stock moves that are executed without being bound to a standard warehouse operation type *(such as Receipts or Delivery Orders)*. Specifically, **Scrap Orders** *(e.g., `SP/00001`)* write off damaged or defective goods directly to `Virtual Locations/Scrap` and appear under **None** to ensure overall inventory throughput balances accurately.

---

### 44. Why do some quantity figures appear in red on the Moves Analysis list register?
In the Moves Analysis list view, quantities displayed in **red text** *(such as `1.00 Units` on delivery `WH/OUT/00069` or `2.00 Units` on scrap `SP/00001`)* signify stock deductions from internal warehouse storage bins (`WH/Stock`). Conversely, quantities displayed in standard dark text represent inbound stock additions into internal storage from supplier receipts (`WH/IN`).

---

### 45. Why does Moves History show 12 rows while Moves Analysis shows 11 rows?
* **Moves Analysis** operates at the high-level transfer order line level, combining total requested demand and total received units per product per order. In Moves Analysis, vendor receipt `WH/IN/00074` appears as a single aggregated row with `Demand: 6.00` and `Quantity: 10.00 Units`.
* **Moves History** operates at the granular detailed operation level. In Moves History, receipt `WH/IN/00074` is split into two physical operations: one for `4.00 Units` and one for `6.00 Units`, representing separate physical pallet staging or storage bin putaways. This increases total record count from 11 to 12.

---

### 46. How does Moves History track split quantities on a single transfer?
When a transfer involves multiple storage bins, separate pallets, or distinct hardware lots, CURQ records each physical move as an independent atomic line in Moves History. Each line captures its own specific timestamp, source and destination locations, lot or serial number identifier, and executed quantity, providing granular operational traceability.

---

### 47. What is the difference between On Hand Quantity and Reserved Quantity in the Locations report?
* **On Hand Quantity**: The total physical count of units stored in that specific warehouse location *(e.g., `50.00 Units` in `WH/Stock`)*.
* **Reserved Quantity**: The portion of on-hand units that CURQ has committed to confirmed outbound delivery orders, manufacturing work orders, or pending internal transfers awaiting physical picking *(e.g., `0.00 Units`)*.
* **Available Quantity**: The remaining stock available to promise for new sales orders, calculated as On Hand Quantity minus Reserved Quantity.

---

### 48. What do the History and Replenishment action links do on a Locations report line?
Each row in the Locations list view features two contextual action shortcuts:
* **History** *(clock counter-clockwise icon)*: Opens the Moves History ledger pre-filtered to that specific product and location, displaying every historical double-entry stock receipt, internal movement, and customer delivery affecting that bin.
* **Replenishment** *(cycle arrows icon)*: Navigates directly to the replenishment rules configuration for that product at that specific location, enabling inventory controllers to modify min/max thresholds, adjust procurement lead times, or trigger immediate replenishment purchase orders.

---

### 49. Does clicking "+ New" on Reporting > Locations create a new warehouse storage location?
No. Clicking **+ New** on the Locations report (`Reporting > Locations`) inserts an inline editable stock balance line used for recording manual opening balances or physical count adjustments in an existing location *(such as `WH/Stock`)*. Full physical location definitions *(such as defining new aisles, shelves, or bins with location types and removal strategies)* are managed exclusively under **Configuration > Locations** when the Storage Locations setting is enabled. The Location field on the balance row restricts direct creation to prevent users from accidentally adding unconfigured bins on the fly from a reporting table.

---

### 50. Why is there no "Create and Edit" option in the Location field dropdown on Reporting > Locations?
CURQ restricts creating new locations directly from the Location field in `Reporting > Locations`. Locations are structural warehouse assets linked to double-entry routing, putaway rules, and removal strategies. Allowing ad-hoc location creation from an inventory adjustment table would risk misspelled bin codes and corrupted warehouse organization. To create new locations, administrators must enable **Storage Locations** under **Configuration > Settings**, then create and manage locations under **Configuration > Locations**.

---

### 51. What is the difference between On Hand, Free to Use, Incoming, and Outgoing in the Stock report?
In the Stock report (`Reporting > Stock`):
* **On Hand**: The physical count of units currently residing in warehouse storage *(e.g., `50.00 Units`)*.
* **Free to Use**: Unreserved units available to promise for new sales orders without causing fulfillment shortages, calculated as On Hand minus Outgoing reservations *(e.g., `50.00 Units`)*.
* **Incoming**: Units scheduled to arrive from approved supplier purchase orders or inbound transfers awaiting dock intake *(e.g., `0.00 Units`)*.
* **Outgoing**: Units committed to confirmed customer delivery orders or manufacturing orders awaiting physical picking *(e.g., `0.00 Units`)*.

---

### 52. How does the Inventory at Date wizard recalculate historical stock balances in the Stock report?
When clicking **Inventory at Date** in the Stock report (`Reporting > Stock`) and confirming a past date and time, CURQ rolls back subsequent double-entry receipts, deliveries, and adjustments. It dynamically reconstructs physical quantities, unit costs, and total inventory valuations exactly as they stood at that historical timestamp, providing point-in-time figures for fiscal audits and tax compliance.
