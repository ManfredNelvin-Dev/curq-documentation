# Website and eCommerce FAQ

Frequently asked questions about website administration, multi-site selection, product publishing, payment providers, and customer portal accounts in CURQ 18.

---

### 1. How do I switch between managing My Website and My Website 2?

In the top header bar of the Website application or configuration screen, click the **Settings of Website** dropdown selector to switch between `My Website` and `My Website 2`. Every page layout, menu structure, and eCommerce setting dynamically filters to the chosen website.

---

### 2. Why does a catalog product like Conference Chair not appear on the shop page?

Open the product template in the backend or visit `/shop` while in edit mode, and verify that the product is toggled to **Published**. Unpublished items remain completely hidden from public website visitors.

---

### 3. Can customers pay for online orders using Wire Transfer?

Yes. Customers can select `Wire Transfer` during checkout to review payment instructions and confirm their order immediately without entering payment card credentials. The resulting order remains in quotation status until accounting validates receipt of funds.

---

### 4. Where do messages submitted through the Contact Us form go?

Submissions through the `/contactus` page generate a new lead record in CRM, automatically routed to the `Website` sales team and assigned to `Admin or Selected Salesperson` for commercial follow-up.

---

### 5. Can a customer choose Pick up in store instead of Standard delivery?

Yes. If `Pick up in store` is enabled under shipping methods, shoppers can select in-store collection at checkout with zero delivery surcharge, and choose the pickup location from the selected warehouse outlet.
