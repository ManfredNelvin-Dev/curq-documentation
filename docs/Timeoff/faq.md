# Time Off FAQ

Frequently asked questions about leave entitlements, approval workflows, request statuses, and calendar configurations in CURQ 18.

---

## Who can access the Management section in the Time Off application?
Only users with **Time Off Officer** or **Administrator** permissions can access the **Management** section. Frontline team managers can access requests submitted by employees they directly supervise, while full administrators can review and approve leave and allocation records across all enterprise departments.

## What is the difference between To Approve and Second Approval?
**To Approve** indicates that a submitted leave request is awaiting its initial review by the employee's direct manager (or HR officer). **Second Approval** indicates that the request has received its first approval under a dual-validation leave type and is waiting for final validation by a Time Off Officer before balances are officially deducted.

## How do I approve or refuse a time-off request?
Navigate to **Time Off → Management → Time Off**. Locate the required request row in the list view and click **Approve** to authorize single-approval requests, **Validate** to complete a second approval, or **Refuse** to reject the request. Alternatively, open the request form view and execute the action from the top control bar.

## How do I view approved or refused allocation requests?
Navigate to **Time Off → Management → Allocations**. By default, the view applies the **First Approval** and **My Team** filters, which hide completed records. Click the clear (`x`) icon next to **First Approval** in the search bar, or select **Approved** or **Refused** from the left-hand status grouping panel.

## Why is a submitted leave request not visible in the Management list?
A request may be hidden by active search filters, such as the default **Waiting For Me** or **My Team** filters. Alternatively, the request may belong to a department or company branch outside your security access scope. Review the search bar, clear restrictive filter tags, and verify that your user account has the appropriate Time Off Officer permissions.

## How does CURQ handle public holidays during an employee's vacation?
If **Public Holiday Included?** is unchecked on the time-off type (the standard default), CURQ automatically excludes configured public holidays from the calculated leave duration. For example, if an employee requests leave from Monday to Friday and Wednesday is a designated public holiday, CURQ deducts exactly 4 working days from their balance.
