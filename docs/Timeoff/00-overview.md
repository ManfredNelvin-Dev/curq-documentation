# Time Off

Manage employee leave requests, track absence balances, automate accrual plans, and streamline multi-tier approval workflows in CURQ 18.

## Seamless Leave Requests and Absence Tracking
With Time Off, employees and managers gain immediate visibility over personal leave entitlements and planned absences from a unified workspace. Employees can submit time-off requests with flexible duration options (full day, half day, or custom hours), request additional allocations, and monitor real-time leave balances across different leave types through interactive dashboard cards and yearly calendar views.

## Multi-Tier Approvals, Allocations, and Flexible Configurations
Time Off equips HR officers and team managers with robust tools to review, approve, validate, or refuse leave requests and allocations across departments. Automate leave entitlement accumulation using customizable accrual plans, assign bulk allocations to teams or employee tags in a single operation, schedule public holidays and mandatory days, and analyze absence trends through comprehensive graphical and pivot reports.
