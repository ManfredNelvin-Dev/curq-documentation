# Solving Leave Approval Delays and Allocation Errors with CURQ 18

## Business Challenge

Managing employee leave becomes increasingly difficult as an organization grows. Employees submit time-off requests, managers need to review them, and HR teams must ensure that leave allocations remain accurate.

When these activities are handled through emails, spreadsheets, or manual follow-ups, several problems can arise:

* **Delayed approvals:** Managers may overlook leave requests, leaving employees uncertain about whether their time off has been approved.
* **Unclear approval responsibilities:** Requests requiring multiple approvals can be delayed when the next approver is unaware that action is needed.
* **Allocation errors:** HR teams may struggle to track how much leave has been allocated to each employee and whether additional allocation requests have been approved.
* **Limited visibility:** Managers may find it difficult to identify pending requests across different departments.
* **Time-consuming administration:** HR staff spend additional time searching for requests, checking statuses, and following up with managers.

These challenges can make leave management less efficient and complicate workforce planning.

---

## The Solution: Centralized Leave Management with CURQ 18

CURQ 18's Time Off application provides a centralized approach to managing employee leave requests and leave allocations. Its Management section enables authorized users to review requests, track their status, and perform the required approval actions from one place.

### 1. Reduce Approval Delays

Managers can access pending employee leave requests through **Time Off → Management → Time Off**.

The default **Waiting For Me** filter helps managers focus on requests that require their approval. Instead of searching through emails or separate spreadsheets, they can review the available requests in a centralized list.

Managers can open each request, review its details, and approve or refuse it as appropriate.

### 2. Handle Multi-Level Approvals

Some organizations require additional authorization before granting employee leave. For example, a company may require a team manager to review a request before a second approver validates it.

CURQ 18 supports approval workflows that can require one or two approvals, depending on the configured time-off type.

Requests awaiting their first approval are marked **To Approve**, while requests requiring another approval are marked **Second Approval**. These statuses help authorized users identify the next action required.

### 3. Improve Leave Allocation Accuracy

Employees may need additional leave allocation according to company policies. Without a structured process, HR teams can find it difficult to track which allocation requests are awaiting approval and which have already been processed.

CURQ 18 provides an allocation management page at **Time Off → Management → Allocations**.

Authorized users can review the requested time-off type and allocation amount before approving or refusing a request. This provides a centralized way to manage allocation requests and review their status.

### 4. Improve Visibility Across Departments

Managers and HR teams may need to review requests from different departments or focus on a particular group of employees.

CURQ 18 provides status and department groupings on its management pages. Users can filter requests to focus on a specific department or status, making it easier to organize approval activities.

If a request is not visible, users can review the active search filters and remove those that unnecessarily restrict the results.

### 5. Simplify Administrative Work

The centralized management views allow authorized users to review requests and perform approval actions without navigating through multiple separate records.

Users can open individual request forms to review additional information and, where their permissions allow, make changes. This helps streamline routine leave administration and makes request handling easier to manage.

---

## Business Benefits

By using CURQ 18's Time Off management features, organizations can improve their leave administration processes in several ways:

* **Better approval visibility:** Pending requests are easier to identify.
* **Clearer approval workflows:** Request statuses indicate whether a first or second approval is required.
* **Improved allocation oversight:** Allocation requests can be reviewed and processed in a centralized location.
* **More efficient request handling:** Status and department filters help managers find relevant records.
* **Reduced manual follow-up:** Centralized request lists reduce the need to rely entirely on separate tracking spreadsheets and email conversations.

The actual improvements depend on the organization's configuration, approval policies, and how consistently managers use the system.

---

## Conclusion

Leave approval delays, allocation errors, and limited visibility can create unnecessary administrative work for HR teams and managers. CURQ 18 addresses these challenges by centralizing time-off requests and allocation management, providing clear approval statuses, and enabling authorized users to filter and process requests efficiently.

With a properly configured approval workflow, organizations can improve oversight of employee leave and establish a more consistent approach to leave administration.
