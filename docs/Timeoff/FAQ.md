# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Frequently asked questions about access permissions, approval workflows, request statuses, and search filters in the CURQ 18 Time Off module.

---

### 1. Who can access the Management section in the Time Off application?

Only users with the appropriate permissions to approve time-off and allocation requests can access the **Management** section. The records they can view depend on their Time Off Officer or Administrator access rights.

---

### 2. What is the difference between To Approve and Second Approval?

**To Approve** indicates that a request is waiting for its first approval. **Second Approval** indicates that the request has received its first approval and requires an additional approval before it can be validated.

---

### 3. How can I approve or refuse a time-off request?

Navigate to **Time Off → Management → Time Off**. Locate the required request and click **Approve** to approve it, **Validate** to complete a second approval, or **Refuse** to reject it.

---

### 4. How can I view approved or refused allocation requests?

Navigate to **Time Off → Management → Allocations**. The page applies the **First Approval** and **My Team** filters by default. Remove the **First Approval** filter and adjust other active filters as needed to view approved and refused allocation requests.

---

### 5. Why can't I see a particular request in the Management list?

The request may be excluded by the active search filters, status selection, or department grouping. Your access rights may also restrict which employees' requests you can view. Review the active filters and remove any that are unnecessarily limiting the results.
