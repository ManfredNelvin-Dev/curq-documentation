# Dashboards FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Standard Dashboards, My Dashboard, layout options, and permissions in CURQ 18.

---

### 1. What is the difference between Standard Dashboards and My Dashboard?

Standard Dashboards are pre-built company dashboards organized by department *(such as Sales, Finance, or Logistics)* with shared filters for all users. **My Dashboard** is your personal workspace where you pin your own custom lists, charts, and shortcuts.

---

### 2. How do I add a report or list to "My Dashboard"?

Open any list or graph in CURQ *(such as Invoices or Delivery Orders)*, click the search bar, open the **Favorites** dropdown menu, and click **Add to my dashboard**.

---

### 3. Can other users see my "My Dashboard" layout?

No. **My Dashboard** is completely private to your login account. Your pinned cards, folded widgets, and column layout changes only appear for you.

---

### 4. Why is a department dashboard missing from my left sidebar?

The dashboard is either restricted to a security access group that your account does not belong to, or an administrator has not checked the **Is Published** box in **Configuration > Dashboards**.

---

### 5. How do I change the number of columns in My Dashboard?

Open **Dashboards > My Dashboard**, click the **Change Layout** button in the top action bar, and select **1 Column**, **2 Columns**, or **3 Columns**.
