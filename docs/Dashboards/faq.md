# Dashboards FAQ

## What is the difference between Standard Dashboards and My Dashboard?
Standard Dashboards are pre-built company-wide dashboards organized by department (such as Sales, Finance, or Logistics) with shared global filters and drill-down analytics for authorized teams. **My Dashboard** is your private operational workspace where you can pin custom lists, Kanban boards, and charts from across CURQ to track your personal daily priorities.

## How do I add a report or list to "My Dashboard"?
Navigate to any List, Kanban, Graph, or Pivot view across CURQ, apply your desired filters, click the **Actions / Settings gear icon** (`⚙`) next to the view title (or the **Favorites** dropdown menu), select **Dashboard**, specify a title for the widget, and click **Add**.

## Can other users see my "My Dashboard" layout?
No. **My Dashboard** is completely private to your user login account. Your pinned widgets, minimized cards, and selected 1, 2, or 3 column layout are stored strictly within your personal user profile and are invisible to other team members.

## Why is a department dashboard missing from my left sidebar?
A dashboard will not appear in the sidebar if it is restricted to a security access group that your account does not belong to, if the active company context does not match the dashboard's assigned company in a multi-company instance, or if an administrator has not checked the **Is Published** checkbox in **Configuration > Dashboards**.

## How do I change the number of columns in My Dashboard?
Open **Dashboards > My Dashboard**, click the **Change Layout** button in the top action bar, and choose between **1 Column**, **2 Columns**, or **3 Columns**. CURQ immediately reorganizes your active widgets into the selected grid structure.
