# CRM and Pipeline FAQ

Frequently asked questions about opportunities, stages, lost reasons, win probabilities, and team assignment in CURQ 18.

---

### 1. What is the difference between an unscrubbed Lead and an Opportunity?

A Lead is an unverified incoming contact *(such as a business card or website inquiry)* that has not yet been qualified. An Opportunity is a verified commercial prospect with a defined deal value, sales stage, and estimated closing date.

---

### 2. What happens when an opportunity is marked as Lost?

The opportunity probability drops to 0%, a red **Lost** ribbon attaches to the record, and the card archives from active Kanban views while recording the selected reason and closing note in the chatter.

---

### 3. Can an archived or lost opportunity be restored if a prospect calls back?

Yes. Open **CRM > Sales > My Pipeline**, click the search bar, select the **Lost** filter, open the archived deal, and click the **Restore** button in the top action bar to return it to active stages.

---

### 4. Can I override the automatic win probability percentage?

Yes. Click directly into the **Probability** field on any opportunity and enter a custom percentage. A small gear icon appears next to the field, allowing you to reset back to the automated system calculation whenever desired.

---

### 5. Why does an opportunity created by a coworker not appear in My Pipeline?

The default pipeline view applies a **My Pipeline** filter that displays only deals assigned to your user account. Remove the **My Pipeline** filter in the search bar or filter by **Sales Team** to view deals managed by other colleagues.
