# CRM FAQ

### 1. What is the difference between a Lead and an Opportunity, and when should Leads be enabled?
A **Lead** is an unverified raw inquiry (web submission, trade show scan), whereas an **Opportunity** is a qualified commercial deal actively managed in your pipeline. Enable the Leads feature in CRM Settings if your team needs a dedicated pre-qualification filter before populating pipeline forecasts.

### 2. Why should lost deals be marked as Lost instead of being deleted?
Deleting opportunities destroys sales performance history and distorts conversion metrics. Marking a deal as **Lost** archives the card while preserving communication notes and quotation history, allowing management to track lost reasons in CRM reports and launch future re-engagement campaigns.

### 3. How does Predictive Lead Scoring calculate deal win probabilities?
CURQ analyzes historical won and lost deals using statistical variables such as pipeline stage, customer geography, contact data completeness, and lead source. To update predictions after process changes, navigate to CRM Settings and click **Retrain Scoring Model**.

### 4. How does automated, rule-based lead assignment distribute prospects to sales reps?
Incoming leads are evaluated against team domain filters (such as country or industry) and assigned via round-robin distribution to team members. The engine checks each salesperson's monthly capacity limit to ensure leads are only routed to reps who have available capacity.

### 5. How does creating a quotation from an Opportunity keep revenue forecasts updated?
Clicking **New Quotation** on an opportunity links commercial offer lines directly to the deal and updates the **Expected Revenue** to match the quotation value. Once the customer accepts and the order is confirmed, the linked opportunity automatically moves to the **Won** stage at 100% probability.
