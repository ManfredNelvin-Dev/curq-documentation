# Audit Logs & Compliance Tracking

Maintaining strict audit trails is essential for internal controls and regulatory compliance. CURQ records system activity across all modules.

---

## 1. Automatic Chatter Tracking
Every document record (Invoices, Quotations, Customer Leads, Journal Entries) maintains a real-time **Chatter Log** at the bottom of the page detailing:
- User identity who created or modified the record.
- Timestamp of modification.
- Field value changes (before and after values).

---

## 2. Accessing Audit Trail Reports
1. Navigate to **Settings > Technical > Audit Logs**.
2. Filter logs by module, user, action type (Create, Update, Delete), or date range.
3. Export log reports for financial audit or compliance verification.
