# Multi-Currency & Localization Guide

CURQ provides global financial capabilities supporting international business operations across different tax jurisdictions and currencies.

---

## 1. Enabling Multi-Currency
1. Go to **Accounting > Settings**.
2. Scroll to the **Currencies** section and check **Multi-Currencies**.
3. Save configuration settings.

---

## 2. Exchange Rate Synchronization
- Exchange rates can be set to update automatically on a daily/weekly schedule from central banks (e.g. European Central Bank, Federal Reserve).
- Manual exchange rate overrides can be entered for custom contracts.

---

## 3. Currency Realized/Unrealized Gain & Loss
When settling foreign invoices or revaluing foreign currency bank accounts at month-end, CURQ automatically generates exchange rate variance journal entries to maintain accurate general ledgers.
