# Share Appointment Availability

This guide covers how to use the Share Availabilities feature in CURQ 18 Calendar to publish booking slots, configure appointment parameters, and generate shareable links for external clients and partners.

---

## Task 1: Select Available Time Slots

When the Appointments integration is active in your database, you can dynamically select and share open booking intervals directly from the calendar:

1. Open **Calendar**.
2. Click the **Share Availabilities** button in the top action bar.
3. Switch to **Day** view (the system allows interactive drag-selection of custom time ranges specifically within Day view).
4. Click and drag across the calendar grid to mark your open windows (e.g., `09:00 - 12:00` and `14:00 - 17:00`).
5. To adjust or remove a selected range, hover over the slot and click the **Trash** icon.
6. Once your open windows are selected, click **Open** to access the linked appointment configuration.

---

## Task 2: Configure Appointment Parameters

In the appointment setup window, configure scheduling rules and booking policies:

1. **Advance Notice**: Set minimum notice rules (e.g., bookings must be made at least 4 hours in advance) to prevent surprise appointments.
2. **Cancellation Rules**: Specify whether attendees can cancel or reschedule, and set cutoff limits.
3. **Resource / User Allocation**: Designate whether availability is tied to specific team members or shared meeting resources (e.g., conference rooms).
4. **Videoconference Provider**: Link an automated online meeting tool (Odoo Discuss, Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams).
5. **Reminders & Confirmations**: Configure automated confirmation emails and pre-meeting reminder notifications.

---

## Task 3: Share or Publish the Booking Link

1. Click **Preview** to inspect the customer-facing booking portal as external attendees will experience it.
2. Click **Share** to copy the unique appointment URL to your clipboard for use in email signatures, direct messages, or commercial proposals.
3. Click **Publish** if you want the appointment type to appear publicly on your company's website booking portal.
