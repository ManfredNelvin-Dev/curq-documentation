# Calendar FAQ

Frequently asked questions about scheduling meetings, attendee invitations, reminders, recurring events, and calendar synchronizations in CURQ 18 Calendar.

---

## 1. How do I schedule a meeting and invite attendees?

Navigate to **Calendar**, switch to the Day, Week, or Month view, and click on your desired time slot. In the quick-create pop-up, enter the meeting summary and click **Edit** (or press Enter) to open the full event form. In the **Attendees** field, select internal colleagues or enter external email addresses. CURQ automatically dispatches email invitations with calendar ICS attachments when the meeting is saved.

---

## 2. Can I configure recurring meetings or repeating events?

Yes. Open or create an event in the **Calendar** form view and check the **Recurrent** checkbox. You can specify the repetition frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly), the repeat interval (e.g., every 2 weeks), specific weekdays, and the recurrence end boundary (either by number of occurrences or a specific end date).

---

## 3. How do reminders and event notifications work?

In the meeting form under the **Reminders** field, you can assign one or more notification rules, such as *Notification 15 minutes before*, *Email 1 hour before*, or *SMS reminder*. When the reminder threshold is reached, CURQ triggers browser push notifications, internal system alerts, or automated email messages to all confirmed attendees.

---

## 4. How do I view other team members' calendars side-by-side?

In the right-hand sidebar of the Calendar view, locate the **Attendees / Everyone's Calendars** section. Check the boxes next to your colleagues' names to overlay their scheduled commitments alongside your own calendar. Each attendee's events are color-coded to easily identify scheduling overlaps and determine mutual availability.

---

## 5. Can I synchronize CURQ Calendar with Google Calendar or Microsoft Outlook?

Yes. CURQ supports two-way synchronization with external calendar providers via Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar integrations configured under **Settings → General Settings → Integrations**. Once configured, events scheduled or modified in CURQ automatically reflect in your external calendar, and vice versa.
